<Aside type="tip" title="The mission">
Build **maths**, a game where two friends race each other at maths on their phones, **by your own hand**, starting from "what is a server?". By the end you'll be able to draw how it works, rebuild its core without looking, and beat your friends at it.
</Aside>

## The game

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Live Duel" icon="rocket">
    Two players get the **same questions in the same order** (`7 × 8`, `63 ÷ 9`, …). You have **60 seconds**, and the most correct answers wins. You can watch your opponent's score climb live.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cross Math Duel" icon="puzzle">
    Both players get the **same grid**, where every row and column is an equation with blanks. The **first correct solve** wins.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How the lessons work
- **Short.** Each lesson takes 15–30 minutes and ends with **one tangible win**: something you ran, built or drew yourself.
- **Knowledge first, then practice.** A small piece of theory, then you *do* it, with quick feedback from quizzes, your terminal, and me.
- **Absolute basics.** No step is too small. If something is unclear, **ask me in the chat**. That's what I'm here for.
- **Recall before new material.** Each lesson opens with questions on the previous ones, answered from memory. Struggling a bit is the point: that's what makes it stick.
- **You write the server code.** I explain, ask, and review; I don't write it for you. The phone app can be AI-built later.
- **Every claim links to a trusted source**, so you don't have to take my word for it.

## The road (rough; it will adjust to how you're doing)

| Part | You'll learn | You'll have |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Foundations** | what a server is, HTTP by hand, ports, what Spring Boot does for you | a Spring Boot app answering `curl` |
| **2 · The duel** | modelling the game, seeded questions, JSON, endpoints, errors | a duel playable with `curl` |
| **3 · Two players at once** | threads, race conditions, locks, the server's clock, safe retries | a duel that's fair under pressure |
| **4 · Live** | polling vs WebSocket, push, disconnects, heartbeats | opponent's score updating live |
| **5 · Cross Math** | grid model, generating puzzles, checking solutions | the second duel mode |
| **6 · Play** | wiring the (AI-built) app to *your* server | a real duel on two phones 🎮 |
